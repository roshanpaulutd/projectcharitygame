01 / HOW TO PLAY✳

Make every
move matter.

  1. 01Guide the water trailArrow keys or WASD steer continuously; tap the arrows or swipe on touch screens. You cannot reverse into your trail.
  2. 02Collect clean waterEach bright drop adds 10 points and one blue trail segment.
  3. 03Avoid pollution and your trailMuddy drops remove two segments and 20 points. Walls and your own trail cost a life.
  4. 04Reach the clean-water goalCollect 8 drops to reach 100% before the 1:15 timer runs out.
